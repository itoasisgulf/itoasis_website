    Questions we hear often

    How is the monthly fee calculated?

    Usually per user or per device, based on what you have and the cover you need. You receive a fixed monthly quote after the audit.

    Do you come on site?

    Most issues are solved remotely. On-site visits are included for problems that need hands on the equipment, within the terms of your plan.

    Is there a long contract?

    Plans run month to month after an initial period agreed in your quote.

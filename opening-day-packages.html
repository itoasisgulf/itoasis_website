Questions we hear often

How early should we involve you?

Ideally before ceilings and walls are closed, so cabling and Wi-Fi points are in the right place. We can still help later, but early planning avoids rework.

Can you work with our fit-out contractor?

Yes. We coordinate directly with your contractor and designer on cable routes, power and rack locations.

Is the price fixed?

After a site visit you receive a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Monthly software and support fees are shown separately.

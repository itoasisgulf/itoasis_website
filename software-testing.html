Software Testing & QA

Catch the bugs before your customers do.

Get a quoteLearn more

We test websites, apps and business systems as an independent team: manual and automated tests, performance and security checks, and clear bug reports your developers can act on.

What’s included

  • Test plans and test cases from your requirements
  • Manual functional and user acceptance testing
  • Automated regression tests
  • Performance and load testing
  • Mobile testing on real iPhone and Android devices
  • Arabic and right-to-left checks

Who it’s for

  • Companies about to launch a website or app
  • Teams rolling out a new ERP or POS
  • Software houses that need independent QA
  • Businesses with recurring bugs after updates

What you get

  1. 01

    A test report with every issue ranked by severity

  2. 02

    Steps to reproduce and screenshots for each bug

  3. 03

    Automated tests you keep for future releases

  4. 04

    A go or no-go view before launch

Platforms we work with

  • Selenium
  • Cypress
  • Playwright
  • Postman
  • JMeter
  • Appium

How we deliver

Survey. Design. Install. Support.

  1. 01

    Site survey & audit

    We visit, review your current systems, network and devices, and list what to keep, fix or replace.

  2. 02

    Solution design & quote

    You get a clear design, a fixed quote and a timeline aligned with your opening or go-live date.

  3. 03

    Installation & setup

    Our engineers install, configure, test and train your team, then hand over full documentation.

  4. 04

    Ongoing support

    Monthly support, monitoring and updates keep everything running as your business grows.

Questions we hear often

Can you test software another company built?

Yes. Independent testing works best when the testers did not write the code.

Do you fix the bugs too?

We can, or we hand clear reports to your developers. You choose.

Is testing a one-off or ongoing?

Either. Many clients keep us for each new release.

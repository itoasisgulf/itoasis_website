Network Configuration

Fast, stable Wi-Fi and networks that just stay up.

Get a quoteLearn more

We design and configure business networks for offices, shops and restaurants: routers, switches, firewalls and Wi-Fi access points placed for full coverage. Guest Wi-Fi is kept separate from your POS and office systems, and everything is labelled and documented.

What’s included

  • Site survey and Wi-Fi coverage planning
  • Routers, firewalls, switches and access points
  • Separate networks for staff, guests, POS and cameras
  • Internet connection set-up and backup links
  • Remote monitoring and firmware updates
  • Network diagram and password handover

Who it’s for

  • Offices with slow or dropping Wi-Fi
  • Restaurants and cafés offering guest Wi-Fi
  • Shops where card terminals lose connection
  • New sites being fitted out

Questions we hear often

Which internet provider should we use?

In the UAE business internet comes from e& or du. We help you choose a plan and configure the line, including a backup connection if needed.

Why separate guest and POS networks?

It protects payment and business systems from devices you do not control, and keeps guest traffic from slowing down your operations.

Can you fix our existing network?

Yes. We audit what is installed, reuse what is good and replace only what causes problems.
